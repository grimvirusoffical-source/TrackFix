Hold a key, then slide one finger on the trackpad. The pointer stays put. The program sends a real mouse wheel or a real button drag.

Scroll. Hold Right Ctrl. Slide up to scroll up. Slide down to scroll down.

Zoom. Hold Right Shift. Slide up to zoom out. Slide down to zoom in. That includes Blender.

Navigate. Hold F8 and slide to orbit. Hold Left Shift as well to pan.

Stop it

  • Windows, Linux, and Chromebook: Ctrl+Shift+F12 pauses.
  • Mac: Ctrl+Shift+F12 pauses. Ctrl+Shift+Q quits.
  • Linux window: press q to quit, h to open this guide again.
